FeedBender - About the fetcher bot

FeedBender is a personal project to create, tweak or combine various news feeds/sources into "custom feeds". The "custom feeds" are primarily for use in my newsreader or as "data-feeds" for use in other personal hobby-projects. But the created "custom feeds" are public and free for others to use if anybody finds them valuable.

The FeedBender fetcher bot usually shouldn't visit each source-feed more than once per hour, but in periods of experimentation and new developments, the frequency might temporarily be higher.

The fetcher bot is NOT a spider crawling any random link found. The bot monitors a few specific URLs. Usually RSS news feeds or news pages. Sometimes it maybe also be caching a related image, but in general it doesn't fetch or harvest anything besides what is needed to create a few very sparse newsfeeds for monitoring new updates. If the bot unexpectedly creates trouble on your site, please contact me directly. Often I will have a profile/account on the site that the bot is monitoring, from which I can be contacted.
